    Where the block sat, minute by minute

    Distance from the team's own goal line, outfield players only. Open play only: dead balls and the 10 seconds after corners and free kicks are removed. Per-minute medians smoothed over 5 minutes. Hover the chart for exact values.

    Average shape

    Each circle is a player's average position in the selected window (keeper dashed). Both teams are drawn attacking left to right. The shaded outline joins the outermost outfield players. Averages smooth out movement, so the team looks more compact here than it ever is in a single moment. Players on for under 30% of the window are left out.

    15-minute breakdown

    Medians per window, in meters. Back line = deepest outfield player. Length = back line to front line. Time = minutes spent in this phase during the window. Stoppage time counts toward the last window of each half.
